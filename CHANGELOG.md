# Changelog

All notable public changes to ConsumableHUD are documented here.

## v0.1.2 — 2026-10-04

Current public release.

- Displays tracked consumable/tool counts from Inventory, Satchel, Sack, and Case.
- Adds city/hub zone allowlist behavior.
- HUD automatically hides outside supported zones.
- Inventory rescanning is skipped while the HUD is hidden.
- Supports the complete San d'Oria, Bastok, Windurst, and Jeuno city complexes, plus Rabao and both Adoulin city zones.
- Preserves the lightweight, read-only design.

## v0.1.1

Accepted predecessor.

- Established the read-only inventory-counting HUD behavior used by v0.1.2.

## v0.1.0

Initial development build.

- Superseded during development and not published as a supported release.
